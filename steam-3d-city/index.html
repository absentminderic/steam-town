About this town

NMC5344 group project by Jingyi, @absentminderic, @Kenetizen, @letitgotia and Vivian. Data: Steam Games Dataset 2025 (Kaggle), store data up to 10 March 2025.

Project files: report (PDF) · report with R code (HTML) · pixel story · project home

Every tower is a bar chart: its height is the share of games in that group with 100,000 or more estimated owners, at one floor per percent. The foundation under the town is all 81,117 games, split into five owner tiers.

In the forecast game each mission is worth 100 points, minus 5 for every floor your guess is off.

3D town models: KayKit City Builder Bits and Medieval Hexagon Pack by Kay Lousberg, CC0. Megastructures, jacarandas, clouds and lighting are generated in code. Pixel icons drawn for this project.

Music: “Not Quite a Masterpiece”, made with Suno.
